# Banoffee Pie Recipe

**Source:** [BBC Good Food - Banoffee Pie](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/banoffee-pie)

## Ingredients

### For the base
- 200g digestive biscuits (about 14), buttered
- 75g melted butter

### For the filling
- 397g can condensed milk
- 300ml double cream
- 2 large bananas
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### For the topping
- 300ml double cream
- 25g icing sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 25g milk chocolate, shaved

## Method

1.  **Make the base:** Crush the digestive biscuits in a food processor until fine. Mix with the melted butter, then press into the base of a 20cm springform cake tin. Chill while you make the filling.

2.  **Make the filling:** Beat the condensed milk, double cream, and vanilla extract together until the cream has thickened and the mixture has doubled in volume. 

3.  **Add bananas:** Slice 1 banana and fold into the mixture. Spoon over the biscuit base and smooth the top. 

4.  **Make the topping:** Whip the cream to soft peaks, then add the icing sugar and vanilla extract and continue whipping until just combined. 

5.  **Assemble and chill:** Spread the whipped cream over the banoffee filling. Thinly slice the remaining banana and arrange on top. Sprinkle with chocolate shavings. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or preferably overnight, before serving.

## Notes
-   This recipe serves 8-10.
-   Preparation time: 30 mins (+ at least 4 hours chilling).
-   The pie needs to be made at least 4 hours in advance to allow it to set properly in the fridge.
